# 10 -LLM Metrics translated

---

Let's review LLM evaluation metrics. First, we have

PEPLEXITY, which measures the uncertainty in the model's predictions. Lower PEPLEXITY

indicates better model fluency. Then, there's BLEU, which evaluates text generation

by comparing N-grams between generated and reference texts, commonly used for machine translation.

RUGE Measures text summarisation quality based on recall of N-grams between generated and reference text.

Exact Match is used in question answering tasks, measuring exact matches to correct answers.

The F1 score balances precision and recall and is often used in classification or QA tasks.

Human evaluation involves subjective assessment of text fluency, coherence and relevance,

especially important for creative tasks. Lastly, the Wiener-Grad Schema Challenge tests reasoning

and natural language understanding beyond text generation.
