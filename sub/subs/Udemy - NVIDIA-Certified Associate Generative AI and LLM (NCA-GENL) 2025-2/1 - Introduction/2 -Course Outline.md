# 2 -Course Outline translated

---

This course is structured tightly around the exam guide and the exam question waiting provided by Nvidia.

We start with the fundamentals of machine learning and AI, including AI workflow, roles and responsibilities,

AI data storage, computing and networking requirements.

We explore GPUs and how Nvidia has driven AI with its reference architectures.

We then work through neural networks and large language models, covering data, pre-processing and feature engineering.

We examine the tools and techniques used and when to apply them, we then delve into the core aspects of training, fine tuning and prompt engineering.

We focus on Nvidia QDAX libraries and solutions such as the NGC, Infineband, high-speed networking, DGX pods, GPUs and the Nvidia AI Enterprise Suite.

We start introducing exam scenarios to help us recognize and apply Nvidia tools and services to gain the best outcome.

We then cover deployment and inference, learning how to apply and use the Nvidia Triton Infrance Server to optimize the delivery of LLM solutions at scale.

We cover sample questions throughout the content, with explanations to help you build muscle memory.

I've included mock exams with more than 200 sample questions to help you get ready for sitting and passing the exam.

Well done for taking your first step towards becoming an LLM practitioner. Let's get started!
