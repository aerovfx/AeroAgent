# 10 - AI Roles and Responsibilities translated

---

In an AI project, various stakeholders play critical roles in ensuring the successful planning, development and operation of AI infrastructure and applications.

AI practitioners such as data scientists, machine learning engineers and AI developers are responsible for designing and building applications that leverage AI to extract meaningful insights from large data sets.

Their work focuses on developing machine learning models, fine-tuning algorithms and deploying solutions to solve real world problems.

Enterprise IT teams are responsible for managing the organization's infrastructure, data and application life cycle.

For them, AI represents an emerging and rapidly evolving workload that demands robust support systems and efficient resource management.

Line of business managers are focused on operational efficiency and maximizing the organization's return on investment in AI initiatives.

Their primary goal is to see AI models deployed into production faster and with measurable impact.

Leaders, including sea level executives and decision makers, are responsible for aligning AI investments with the organization's strategic vision.

They seek to ensure that AI initiatives deliver measurable ROI and position the company for long-term success.
